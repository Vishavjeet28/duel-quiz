Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to "Duel — Daily Quiz Challenge". This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our App.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Phone number, username, and display name.
  • Gameplay Data: Quiz answers, time taken, scores, rank, and match history.
  • Financial Data: Wallet balances (Duel Points). For regulatory compliance, we may collect KYC information (such as ID proof) if you reach the threshold for real-money withdrawals.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and app usage statistics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Operate the game, calculate scores, and maintain the leaderboards.
  • Process virtual currency transactions and manage your wallet.
  • Ensure Fair Play and detect fraudulent activity or cheating.
  • Comply with legal obligations, including tax deductions and KYC requirements for real-money gaming.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting (e.g., Render), database providers, and SMS gateway providers.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights, prevent fraud, or ensure user safety.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data. All API communications are encrypted, and database access is strictly controlled. However, no electronic transmission is 100% secure.

6. User Rights

You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal data. You can initiate an account deletion request directly through the App settings.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
